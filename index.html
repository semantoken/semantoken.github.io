SemanTok: Predictable Semantic Tokens for Efficient Autoregressive Video Generation

Flexible video tokenizers let an autoregressive (AR) model stop at any token budget. SemanTok makes the first tokens carry what the clip is about, so they are cheaper to predict, and a small AR model matches or beats a VideoFlexTok model 3.4× its size.

How it works

A coarse-to-fine tokenizer turns each latent frame into up to 256 tokens; any prefix of k tokens decodes to a video. An AR model then predicts only the prefix the application asks for.

DINO inFrozen DINOv2 features are fed into the encoder next to the video latents.
DINO out, from the prefix onlyTwo light heads must rebuild the DINO features from each kept token prefix, never seeing the noisy latent.
Nothing else changesSame codebook, sequence length, decoder, and AR recipe as VideoFlexTok.

No loss assigns semantics to specific tokens. Nested dropout alone pushes the most useful information into the earliest ones.

Full architecture

Orange: the VideoFlexTok path (VAE → time-causal encoder → FSQ → nested dropout → time-causal flow decoder, trained with flow matching + decoder REPA). Purple: SemanTok's additions: DINOv2 patches concatenated to each VAE patch, the DINO class token added to the first register token, and Dense / Class DINO heads that cross-attend to the kept prefix of frames ≤ t (cosine losses, weight 0.5 each).

Seven findings

We compare the two tokenizers across seven AR sizes (49M–2.29B) and all token budgets, on class-to-video (Kinetics-600) and text-to-video (uCO3D), measuring fidelity (gFVD, gFID) and semantic alignment (class accuracy, ViCLIP, ClipV).

1High semantic alignment and fidelity at every AR size 2Scaling the AR model further improves fidelity 3Semantics hold on out-of-distribution classes 4Higher decoder-REPA alignment at every noise level 5Strong in reconstruction and in generation 6Earlier tokens are cheaper to predict and carry the gain 7One token per frame is too few for every objective

1

SemanTok exhibits high semantic alignment and video fidelity at every AR model size.

  • With each tokenizer at its best k, on Kinetics-600: gFVD −11–24%, gFID −5–17%, class accuracy +25–61%, largest for the smallest models. On uCO3D: +2–13%, +5–8% and +22–30%.
  • This holds on both datasets, and also in gFVD on Kinetics-600. On uCO3D it matches that model in gFVD and beats it in class accuracy.
  • In class accuracy, ClipV and ViCLIP, on both datasets. Kinetics-600 class accuracy: 0.631 vs 0.560 for the 2.29B VideoFlexTok model.
  • More tokens cost more AR compute and eventually hurt gFVD. VideoFlexTok degrades after k=16, SemanTok only after k=32.
2

Increasing SemanTok model size further improves fidelity.

  • From 49M to 2.29B, SemanTok's best-k gFVD falls from 224 to 202 on Kinetics-600 and from 218 to 197 on uCO3D. Scale mostly trims the error that compounds during the token rollout.
  • SemanTok's best-k class accuracy and ClipV lie above VideoFlexTok at every size. The advantage is largest where AR capacity is scarce: at k=64 the gFID gain halves from 49M to 2.29B.
  • 1.33B model on Kinetics-600: SemanTok's gFVD lead shrinks from 28% to 9% by 26B tokens, then holds at 11–14%. Its class-accuracy lead is still 30% after 65.5B tokens.
  • Playing guitar at 201M and 2.29B. At 201M, SemanTok degrades slowly over time, while VideoFlexTok is sharp only at t=1.

3

SemanTok is able to maintain semantic alignment over out-of-distribution classes.

  • At k=16 on uCO3D, SemanTok's reconstructions score higher ClipV and class accuracy than VideoFlexTok's, but about 2.7 dB lower PSNR.
  • 201M AR model at k=16: class accuracy +24% on ID clips and +29% on OOD classes, ClipV better on both. ViCLIP favours SemanTok from k=16 on, but not at k=4.

4

SemanTok achieves higher decoder-REPA semantic alignment at all noise levels, including pure noise.

  • Existing flexible tokenizers align an early decoder layer with DINOv2. But that layer also sees the partly noised latent, which at low noise meets the target almost regardless of the tokens. SemanTok's heads read only the tokens, so only the tokens can lower their loss.
  • At k=32 on uCO3D, SemanTok from pure noise (0.718) matches VideoFlexTok given a 75%-clean latent (0.716) (circled).
  • At k=256, a cleaner latent adds almost nothing to SemanTok's readout, but nearly 9× more to VideoFlexTok's.
5

SemanTok has high fidelity on reconstruction as well as generation.

  • VideoFlexTok wins on reconstruction PSNR and rFVD, yet its realization gap, from reconstruction to generation, is wider. Detail that the AR model cannot predict never reaches a generated video.
  • So a better reconstruction score alone does not rank tokenizers for AR generation.
  • On Kinetics-600 the AR model is conditioned on the class label, which a reconstruction never sees.
6

SemanTok's generation fidelity gain comes from its earlier tokens, which are cheaper to predict.

  • With nothing forced, SemanTok's gFVD is 23% lower at 201M. Once the first 16–64 ground-truth tokens are forced, the lead vanishes, and VideoFlexTok's better-reconstructing tail edges ahead.
  • 8.8 vs 12.9 bits at 201M. The marginal entropy is only ~1 bit lower, so most of the saving comes from context: the tokens are easier to predict from what came before. It is not repetition; SemanTok repeats tokens less often overall.
  • SemanTok's first 4 tokens cost 37 bits per frame, yet nearly match the class accuracy of VideoFlexTok's first 32 tokens (405 bits). Later tokens restore part of the detail, and the generative decoder fills in the rest.
7

SemanTok with one token per frame struggles to serve every objective.

  • On Kinetics-600, SemanTok is never significantly worse. Its fidelity lead takes over at larger budgets (①).
  • At k=1, the pure-noise lead is only 0.01 DINOv2 cosine, vs 0.04–0.07 from k=16. With a mostly clean latent (σ=0.25), SemanTok's readout is lower up to k=4 on uCO3D and k=8 on Kinetics-600.
  • SemanTok keeps its class-accuracy lead at every budget and every AR size.

Limitation. Because SemanTok puts semantics before reconstruction, at small token budgets it does not always reproduce exact colours and appearance details.
